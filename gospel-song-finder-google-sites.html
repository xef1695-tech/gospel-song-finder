About lyrics & copyright

The built-in collection contains public-domain hymn texts with simple illustrative chord arrangements. Online search uses the iTunes Search API to find music catalog entries; it does not supply full lyrics or chord sheets. Copyrighted lyrics and arrangements are shown only when you connect a provider that has authorized display rights. A song being available online does not make its lyrics free to copy.
